# CSA share packing protocol

> Standard operating procedure for Saturday pack day. Linked from the [operations dashboard](/b6f29352-0ba0-456e-a3e8-f230a1a12a8d).

## Before you start

- Confirm the share count for the week with Marina (operations).
- Pull the pack list from the CSA roster.
- Stage the bins, twist-ties, and crate labels at the south door.

## Step-by-step

1. **Wash & weigh.** Salad greens 8 oz, root vegetables 1.5 lb, alliums 4 oz. Discard anything bruised at this step — it's cheaper than the complaint email.
2. **Layer.** Heaviest at the bottom (roots, garlic). Greens go on top so the leaves aren't crushed during transport.
3. **Label.** Apply the route sticker (Mendocino / Fort Bragg / Ukiah) to the crate handle, not the lid — lids get swapped between crates and the sticker walks.
4. **Stage.** Crates by route, alphabetical by member last name. Aresco picks up Mendocino + Fort Bragg at 09:00; Ukiah goes out in the truck with Jorge at 09:30.
5. **Manifest.** Photograph each crate's contents; upload to `/farm/manifests/<date>`. The photo IS the proof-of-pack if a member disputes the share.

## Checklist (today)

- [ ] Member count confirmed against last night's roster
- [ ] All allergy / dietary exceptions reviewed (Sara M = no alliums, Tom V = no nightshades)
- [ ] Cooler temp logged before the pickup window opens
- [ ] Empty crates from last week returned to the stack

## When something goes wrong

If a member's allergy exception isn't on the printed list, **do not substitute** without checking the CSA-allergies sheet. We would rather skip the share than ship the wrong thing.

If a crate count comes up short on staging, stop and recount before pickup. The driver will not wait, and a missing crate at the dropoff is on us.
