# 2026 vegetable season plan

Owner: Pat | Last review: 2026-04-08 | Cert constraints: NOFA-NY organic

12 acres in vegetables this year, split across four fields by rotation
group. Goal: 84 CSA shares × 20-week season + Wednesday/Saturday
Ithaca Farmers' Market + small wholesale to Greenstar Co-op.

## Field assignments

| Field | Acres | Crop group | Lead crops |
|---|---:|---|---|
| North-A | 3.5 | Solanaceae | tomatoes (heirloom + paste), peppers, eggplant |
| North-B | 2.8 | Cucurbits | zucchini, summer squash, winter squash, pie pumpkins |
| South-A | 2.7 | Brassicas + alliums | broccoli, cabbage, kale, leeks, fall garlic |
| South-B | 3.0 | Roots + legumes | carrots, beets, peas, beans, salad greens |

## Planting schedule

| Window | Crops | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mar 20 – Apr 5 | early greens, peas, onion sets | under low tunnels in South-A |
| Apr 15 – May 1 | brassica transplants, potatoes | wait for Mike's go-ahead on last frost date |
| May 5 – May 25 | tomatoes, peppers, eggplant, squash, cukes | TRANSPLANT after May 15 unless protected |
| Jun 1 – Jun 20 | succession beans, beets, lettuce | bi-weekly succession |
| Jul 1 – Aug 5 | fall brassicas, fall greens, storage onions | start in tray, transplant ~3 weeks later |
| Sep 1 – Sep 25 | garlic, cover crops | rye + vetch on harvested fields |

## Inputs ordered

- Seeds: Fedco (90% of order), Johnny's (specialty), High Mowing
  (open-pollinated varieties). Total YTD spend $3,140.
- Compost: 22 yards from the dairy operation (transferred from
  livestock side, not purchased).
- Mulch: 6,000 sq ft of biodegradable plastic mulch from Dubois Agri
  -- on order, delivery April 22.

## Risks

- **Late blight.** Tomato + potato exposure on North-A. Mitigation
  in [Blight watch](/demo-farm/crops/blight-watch-may).
- **Labor.** Need 4 seasonal hands by June 1. Two confirmed, two open.
  See [Seasonal onboarding](/demo-farm/people/seasonal-onboarding) for
  contracts and orientation flow.
- **Irrigation.** North-A drip line from the new pump (post-April-12
  replacement) is under-tested at scale. Run a full-pressure soak
  the first week of May.

## CSA targets

- 84 shares × 20 weeks = 1,680 share-weeks.
- Average box value target: $32 (CSA cost $640/season).
- Weekly variety target: 7–9 items, at least 2 storage-capable, at
  least 1 herb/aromatic.
