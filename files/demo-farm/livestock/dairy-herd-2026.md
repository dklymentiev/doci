## Vaccination schedule

- Spring: BVD, IBR, BRSV, PI3 (5-way modified live) -- done 2026-04-04
- Summer: ScourGuard (pre-calving cows only)
- Fall: Vibrio/Lepto (heifers + cows)

## Routine health

Milk samples sent quarterly to Cornell QMPS for mastitis screening.
Calf scour protocol in `livestock/calf-scour-response.md`.
