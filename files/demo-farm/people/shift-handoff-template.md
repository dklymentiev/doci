`people/handoffs/YYYY-MM-DD-<shift>.md`. The next shift reads it before
starting their morning checklist.

## How to write a good handoff

- **Be specific.** "Tractor sounds weird" is not a handoff. "T-01
  Kubota: high-pitched whine from hydraulic side, started after the
  3pm North-B run, stopped when engine off, came back at restart"
  is a handoff.
- **Less is more.** If nothing happened, say so in two lines and
  stop typing.
- **Don't editorialise.** The handoff is "what happened," not "what I
  think we should do." Recommendations go in the next morning's
  conversation or as a thread on the relevant doc.
- **Photograph anything ambiguous.** Photos to `people/handoffs/photos/`
  with a filename pointing back to the handoff.
