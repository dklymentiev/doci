# Seasonal worker onboarding

Owner: Sarah | Last review: 2026-04-18

Sequence to follow when a new seasonal hand starts. Don't skip steps,
even if the person already worked here last year -- regulations
change, procedures change, and we'd rather be 30 minutes redundant
than miss something.

## Before day one

- [ ] Offer letter signed (template in `people/forms/offer-letter-template.md`).
- [ ] W-4 and I-9 forms ready for first-day completion.
- [ ] Workers' comp registered with NYSIF (within 24 hours of start
      under NY agricultural labor law -- not optional).
- [ ] Schedule first-day check-in (90 min, with Sarah or Mike).
- [ ] Add to contact list with phone, emergency contact, and any
      relevant medical info (allergies, conditions).

## Day one

### 8:00 -- Paperwork (45 min, kitchen table)

- W-4, I-9 (need ID -- check the list: passport OR driver's license + SSN card).
- NY State LS 309 wage notice (must give in writing, on paper, in
  the worker's preferred language if not English).
- Confidentiality and safety acknowledgement (template in `people/forms/`).
- Bank info for direct deposit (we pay every other Friday).

### 8:45 -- Farm tour (45 min)

Walk every building, both fields. Show:

- Where the first aid kits are (barn, packing shed, house mudroom).
- Where the fire extinguishers are.
- Bathroom and break-room.
- Drinking water station (top of barn).
- Property boundary -- where they shouldn't go without us.

### 9:30 -- Equipment intro (60 min)

- No-one operates the tractor on day one. Period.
- Demonstrate: walk-behind tiller, drip irrigation cutoff valves,
  electric fence controller, cooler keys.
- Hand on, then watch them do each, then sign off in
  `people/equipment-signoff/<year>/<name>.md`.

### 10:30 -- Break (15 min)

### 10:45 -- Safety briefing (45 min)

- PPE: gloves, sun protection, water rules, heat policy
  (mandatory shade break every 90 min above 88°F).
- Bee stings, ticks, snake encounters -- protocol.
- Pesticide-free operation: what NOT to bring onto the farm.
- Sharps, cuts, deep wounds: stop work, get to first aid, page Sarah.

### 11:30 -- Lunch (45 min, with the team)

Sit with everyone. Sandwiches and whatever's in the kitchen. This
is the part most new people remember.

### 12:15 -- Shadow Pat or Devon (rest of day)

Pair them with whoever owns the area they'll mostly work in. Don't
leave them solo on day one even for "easy" tasks.

### End of day

- [ ] 10-minute check-in: how did it go, what was unclear, what's
      the worry?
- [ ] Write down anything that came up that's not in this doc, so we
      can add it for next time.

## Day two through end of week

- Pair work continues for at least 3 days.
- Solo tasks only after explicit sign-off (Sarah or Mike).
- End-of-day handoff using the [shift handoff template](/demo-farm/people/shift-handoff-template).
- Friday end-of-week 15-minute conversation: any concerns,
  feedback on the work, schedule for next week.

## What we want them to know without being told

- Mistakes get reported immediately. We do not punish honest
  mistakes; we punish hidden ones.
- Ask "stupid" questions. We will not roll our eyes.
- Lunch is sacred. If we tell you to skip it, we are wrong, push
  back.

## Recurring (every two weeks)

- Check-in with each seasonal worker, 15 min, off the clock if
  they prefer. Notes in `people/check-ins/<year>/<name>.md`.
